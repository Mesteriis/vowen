## Users and purpose
Adult members of a private, operator-provisioned self-hosted relationship workspace.
Daily work: agreed rules, scheduled rituals, reports and explicit review. Guests answer
invitation-scoped forms without becoming members. Source: docs/PRODUCT_SPEC.md.

## Capabilities and constraints
FastAPI/PostgreSQL, Vue Mini App and native Compose Android. No public registration,
role-based global authority, group feed, inferred diary access, automatic punishment,
fabricated points or online state. Each screen uses current server ACL-filtered data.
